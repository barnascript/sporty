⚽ Slip Booker

Upload a tips screenshot → read every pick → open them one tap at a time on your bookmaker to build the slip. Nothing is staked; you confirm each bet yourself.
Click, paste (⌘/Ctrl+V), or drop the screenshot Everything runs in your browser — no image or data is uploaded to any server.
#TeamMarket

How it works: the picture is read on your device. Pick your bookmaker, then use “Open pick” to step through — each tap opens that team’s matches on the bookmaker in a new tab, where you add the market shown and it goes onto your slip. When you’ve added them all, the bookmaker’s slip gives you the booking code. No stake is placed here and you confirm every bet yourself.
